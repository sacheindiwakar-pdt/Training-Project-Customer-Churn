Query 1: Churn Rate by Contract Type
Business Question

Which contract type has the highest customer churn rate?

SQL
SELECT
    dc.contract_name,
    AVG(f.churn) AS churn_rate
FROM fact_customer_account f
JOIN dim_contract dc
    ON f.contract_key = dc.contract_key
GROUP BY dc.contract_name
ORDER BY churn_rate DESC;

Interpretation:
Customers on Month-to-month contracts typically have the highest churn rate, indicating that longer-term contracts improve customer retention.

Query 2: Churn Rate by Payment Method
Business Question

Which payment method is associated with the highest customer churn?

SQL
SELECT
    dp.payment_method,
    AVG(f.churn) AS churn_rate
FROM fact_customer_account f
JOIN dim_payment dp
    ON f.payment_key = dp.payment_key
GROUP BY dp.payment_method
ORDER BY churn_rate DESC;

Interpretation:
Customers using Electronic Check generally show a higher churn rate compared to customers using automatic payment methods.

Query 3: Average Monthly Charges by Churn Status
Business Question

Do customers who churn pay higher monthly charges than customers who stay?

SQL
SELECT
    churn,
    AVG(monthly_charges) AS avg_monthly_charges
FROM fact_customer_account
GROUP BY churn;

Interpretation:
If churned customers have a higher average monthly charge, pricing may be a contributing factor to customer attrition.

Query 4: Top Risky Customer Segment
Business Question

How many customers belong to the highest-risk segment: Month-to-month contract, tenure less than 12 months, and monthly charges above average?

SQL (Count)
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS risky_customer_count
FROM fact_customer_account f
JOIN dim_contract dc
    ON f.contract_key = dc.contract_key
WHERE dc.contract_name = 'Month-to-month'
  AND f.tenure < 12
  AND f.monthly_charges >
      (
          SELECT AVG(monthly_charges)
          FROM fact_customer_account
      );

SQL (List Customers)
SELECT
    f.customer_id,
    f.tenure,
    f.monthly_charges,
    dc.contract_name
FROM fact_customer_account f
JOIN dim_contract dc
    ON f.contract_key = dc.contract_key
WHERE dc.contract_name = 'Month-to-month'
  AND f.tenure < 12
  AND f.monthly_charges >
      (
          SELECT AVG(monthly_charges)
          FROM fact_customer_account
      );

Interpretation:
These customers represent the most vulnerable segment and should be prioritized for retention programs.

Query 5: High-Value Churned Customers
Business Question

How many high-value customers have churned, and what was their average tenure before leaving?

SQL
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS customer_count,
    AVG(tenure) AS avg_tenure
FROM fact_customer_account
WHERE churn = 1
  AND total_charges > 2000;

Interpretation:
This identifies customers who contributed significant revenue before churning, helping the business focus on retaining valuable customers.
